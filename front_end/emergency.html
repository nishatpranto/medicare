First Aid: What To Do Before Reaching the Hospital

🩸 Severe Bleeding +

Apply firm, direct pressure on the wound with a clean cloth. Keep the injured area raised above heart level if possible. Do not remove the cloth even if soaked; add another layer on top. Call for emergency help immediately.

🔥 Burns +

Cool the burn under running water for at least 10 minutes. Do not apply ice, butter, or ointments. Cover loosely with a clean, non-fluffy cloth. Remove tight items like rings before swelling starts.

🦴 Suspected Fracture +

Keep the injured area still, do not try to realign the bone. Support the area with a splint or padding if available. Apply a cold pack to reduce swelling. Avoid moving the person unless necessary.

🫁 Choking +

Encourage the person to cough forcefully. If they cannot breathe, speak, or cough, give 5 back blows between the shoulder blades followed by 5 abdominal thrusts (Heimlich maneuver). Repeat until the object is dislodged or help arrives.

❤️ Suspected Heart Attack +

Call emergency services immediately. Make the person sit down and rest in a comfortable position. Loosen tight clothing. If prescribed, help them take their own aspirin or nitroglycerin. Begin CPR only if they become unresponsive and stop breathing normally.

🚗 Road Accident +

Ensure the area is safe before approaching. Do not move the injured person unless there is immediate danger, to avoid worsening spinal injuries. Check breathing and responsiveness. Control visible bleeding and keep them warm while waiting for medical help.

🥴 Unconsciousness / Fainting +

Lay the person on their back and elevate the legs slightly if there is no injury suspected. Check breathing. If unconscious and breathing, place in the recovery position on their side. Loosen tight clothing and keep them warm until they recover or help arrives.

This guidance is for general awareness only and does not replace professional medical advice. Always contact emergency services for serious situations.
